# **Eikyuu Central: Entertainment Primary Source Matrix**

To maintain an "S-Tier" legitimacy grade in your Entertainment Intelligence section, your AI should prioritize data and reports from these specific industry "Trades" and primary repositories.

## **1\. The Hollywood "Big Three" (The Gold Standard)**

These are the primary sources that Hollywood agents, lawyers, and studio heads use. If it’s not here, it’s usually a rumor.

* **Variety:** The ultimate industry paper. Best for business deals, IPOs, and high-level casting.  
* **The Hollywood Reporter (THR):** Focuses heavily on legal battles, internal studio politics, and "The Power 100" style intelligence.  
* **Deadline Hollywood:** The fastest for "Breaking News" regarding greenlit projects and production dates.

## **2\. Indian Entertainment Nodes (South Asian Primary)**

To capture Bollywood (Hindi) and Regional (Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada) industry intelligence accurately:

* **Box Office India:** The absolute gold standard for raw financial data and trade analysis in the Indian market.  
* **Pinkvilla:** The fastest node for casting scoops and production starts in Mumbai. High-velocity primary reporting.  
* **Film Companion:** Managed by credible critics; best for verified long-form interviews and director-level production insights.  
* **Galatta Plus:** The primary authority for South Indian cinema (Tollywood, Kollywood) intelligence and critical synthesis.  
* **Bollywood Hungama:** Technical trade data including satellite rights, music deals, and distribution logistics.

## **3\. East Asian Intelligence (Primary Nodes)**

To get the jump on K-Drama and Anime news before the Western press translates it:

* **Natalie.mu (Japan):** The absolute gold standard for Japanese entertainment. If a director is interviewed or a production is announced, it happens here first.  
* **Oricon News (Japan):** Primary data for music charts, physical sales, and mainstream Japanese celebrity news.  
* **Naver Entertainment (Korea):** The central hub for all Korean agency press releases (HYBE, SM, YG).  
* **Dispatch (Korea):** Investigative reports on contracts and legal disputes are mathematically precise and primary.

## **4\. Production & Financial Telemetry**

For your "Box Office Telemetry" and "Production Leaks":

* **Production Weekly:** A subscription-based service used by film crews. This is where "Real Leaks" come from.  
* **Box Office Mojo / The Numbers:** Global financial tracking datasets for your sidebar telemetry.  
* **Variety Intelligence Platform (VIP+):** Deep-dive data on streaming churn and ad-tier performance.

## **5\. Award Season Intelligence**

* **GoldDerby:** The primary source for "Voter Sentiment" and expert predictions for global ceremonies.  
* **Puck News (Entertainment Desk):** Insider intelligence that usually moves the needle on studio stocks.

## **6\. Verification Logic for AI Agents**

When your AI processes these sources, it should assign L-Scores as follows:

* **100% (Verified):** Official SEC filings or Press Releases on official agency portals (e.g., Yash Raj Films, Disney, HYBE).  
* **90-95% (Corroborated):** A story reported by at least two of the "Big Three" OR a trade-verified report from Box Office India.  
* **70-80% (Leak):** Reports from *Production Weekly* or *Pinkvilla* based on internal memos/set leaks.  
* **Below 60% (Speculative):** Social media rumors or unverified fan blogs.
